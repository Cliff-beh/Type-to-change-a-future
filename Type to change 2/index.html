Why Type to change a future?

Closing the digital divide by transforming an everyday computer skill into life-changing educational opportunities.

The Digital Divide is an Opportunity Divide

In today's digital world, basic computer access is essential for learning, research, and future employment. Yet, thousands of underprivileged children in our communities have never owned or operated a computer.

Without digital access, these bright young minds fall behind in education and digital literacy. Team Beyond the Ordinary launched Type to change a future to provide refurbished laptops, sponsored internet access, and digital literacy workshops for children in need.
